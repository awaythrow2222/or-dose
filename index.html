Weight formulas

Ideal body weight (Devine)
Male 50 kg, female 45.5 kg, plus 2.3 kg per inch over 60 in. Requires height ≥ 140 cm. If TBW is below IBW, TBW is used.
Adjusted body weight
IBW + 0.4 × (TBW − IBW). Equals TBW when TBW ≤ IBW.
Lean body weight (Janmahasatian)
Male: 9270·W / (6680 + 216·BMI). Female: 9270·W / (8780 + 244·BMI).
BSA (Mosteller)
√(height cm × weight kg / 3600).
Maintenance fluid (4-2-1)
4 mL/kg/hr for the first 10 kg, 2 mL/kg/hr for the next 10 kg, 1 mL/kg/hr above 20 kg.

Pediatric estimates

Weight from age
Neonate 3.5 kg; 1–11 months: months/2 + 4; 1–10 years: 2 × (age + 4); over 10 years: 3 × age.
Endotracheal tube
Cuffed: age/4 + 3.5. Uncuffed: age/4 + 4. Rounded to 0.5 mm (ties down). Infants and neonates use age/weight tables.
ETT depth at lip
Neonate: weight + 6. Infant: 3 × ETT size. Child: age/2 + 12 cm.
LMA size
By weight: <5 kg #1, 5–10 #1.5, 10–20 #2, 20–30 #2.5, 30–50 #3, 50–70 #4, 70–100 #5, >100 #6.
